Rings

No ring yet. Press + Add rings, pick one ring, and the chooser opens again for the next one. Cancel it when done.

Tips

  • Keep the rings close to the computer or phone (less than 1 m).
  • Close the other apps using a ring: a connected ring doesn't show in the chooser.
  • Rings are updated one at a time (about 10 s each on LibRing firmware, 45 s on a stock ring).
  • A failed or interrupted update is safe: the ring keeps its previous firmware. Just try again.
  • Stock rings (never updated) only take the image of their model: the page tries the 2026 rings' image (0x4C), then the older one (0x17).
  • After an update, a ring keeps its name. A ring that had a factory address (stock rings, a few old ones) gets a new name LibRing_XX: add it again.
  • Battery and signal: read over a short connection when a ring is added (Refresh info reads them again). The firmware version shows before an update only in browsers that pass the rings' advertisements to the page (not Android Chrome, not Chrome on Linux). The address is hidden by every browser: only its last byte (the ring's name) is shown; make scan shows it all.
  • Android: the chooser opens once per click: press + Add another ring for each ring.
  • Linux: Chrome needs chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features. If connections keep dropping: ConnectionSupervisionTimeout=400 in /etc/bluetooth/main.conf.
  • iPhone / iPad: use the Bluefy browser. From a computer you can also use make flash_all (see the README).
