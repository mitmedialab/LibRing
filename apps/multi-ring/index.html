Signal strength between each ring and this computer (RSSI)

Connected rings measure it themselves and send it with the accelerometer data (firmware of 2026-11-11 or later). Rings that aren't connected are measured from their advertisements by this computer, with "Scan RSSI" (not available in every browser). "Ignored": readings below −100 dBm that a ring reports now and then, weaker than anything its radio can receive (about −94 dBm), probably taken when no packet came in: left out of the RSSI and the graph.
