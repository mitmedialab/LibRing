R = (AC/DC of red) / (AC/DC of blue) over the last 10 s: AC is the standard deviation of the 0.7–3.5 Hz band, DC the mean. Shown only while the heart-rate confidence is above 50%. A = 100 and B = 5 are placeholders: they must be fitted against a real pulse oximeter (record R next to it at several SpO2 levels and fit a line), for this camera, this light and this person.

Webcam SpO2 is not reliable: the camera's colour filters, white balance, auto exposure, the light's spectrum, skin tone and motion all change R, and errors of several % remain even after calibration. This is not a medical or reference measurement.
