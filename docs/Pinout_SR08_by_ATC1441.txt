LED matrix (added by LibRing, see Firmware_src/.../prox_reporter/src/leds.c):
6 lines x 7 segments, multiplexed one line at a time.
  Lines (theLedGpiosHigh, line 0-5):    P2_8 P2_7 P2_6 P2_5 P2_2 P2_9
  Segments (theLedGpiosLow, bit 0-6):   P2_1 P1_3 P2_0 P1_2 P1_0 P1_1 P0_2
  Lines 0-4: the 5 digits (bit 0 A top, 1 F top left, 2 B top right,
             3 G middle, 4 E bottom left, 5 C bottom right, 6 D bottom).
  Line 5 (P2_9): the indicators on the outside of the ring:
    bit 4 (P2_9 + P1_0): red heart
    bit 6 (P2_9 + P0_2): green steps
    (bits 0-3 and 5: nothing seen)
